Forschungsgruppe Wahlen · Politbarometer

Federal Polling Trends

Every Politbarometer projection since 1977, by party and by bloc. Lines are smoothed with a 30-day exponentially weighted average; the vertical rules mark federal elections.

Data

Current: Forschungsgruppe Wahlen Politbarometer (© Forschungsgruppe Wahlen / GESIS — terms of use)

Historical: Forschungsgruppe Wahlen, Mannheim (2026). Politbarometer 1977-2024 (Partial Cumulation) (ZA2391; Version 17.0.0) [Data set]. GESIS, Cologne. doi.org/10.4232/1.14611

Election results: Bundeswahlleiterin — official final second-vote shares, shown in the tables' election rows

Method

Trend lines are smoothed with an exponentially weighted average at a 30-day halflife: a poll from 30 days ago carries half the weight of today's.

Blocs are summed from the party series — left is SPD + Grüne + Die Linke, right is CDU/CSU + AfD, everything else is other.
