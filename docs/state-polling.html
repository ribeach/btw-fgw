Method

Left is SPD + Grüne + Die Linke. Right is CDU/CSU + AfD.

Each state averages its recent surveys, weighted by sample size and decaying by half every 30 days. States with no current survey fall back to their last state election result.

The national summary weights the 16 states by population, not by survey count.
